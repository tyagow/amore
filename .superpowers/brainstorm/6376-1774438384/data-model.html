Two tables: rules (with versioning) and events (compliance tracking)

Key Design Decisions

Versioning

rule_group_id stays same across amendments. Query latest version with MAX(version) WHERE rule_group_id = X

Streaks

Computed from rule_events. Count consecutive days with at least one "followed" event and no "broken" events.

Couple Score

SUM(points) from rule_events for the couple. Cooperative — both partners share the total.

AI Detection

ai_detectable + ai_detection_hint tell the coach what to look for in WhatsApp messages.
